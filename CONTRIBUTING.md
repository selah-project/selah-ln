# Kosangana na libongoli ya lingala ya Selah

Batangi, bayekoli ya ebre, babongoli mpe moto nyonso oyo alobaka lingala uta bomwana — boyambami. Bino boyebi lokota koleka masini. Mokanda oyo elobi ndenge ya kosunga.

## Koyebisa mbeba to kopesa likanisi

- Fungola **Issue** na ebombelo: <https://github.com/selah-project/selah-ln>
- To, soki oyebi kobongisa yo moko, fungola **Pull request**.
- To komela biso: <support@selahproject.com>

## Nini otya na liyebisi

- Esika: mokanda, mokapo, vɛrsɛ (ndakisa: `genesis 1:1`).
- Oyo ezali sikoyo, mpe mpo na nini ezali mbeba.
- Oyo esengeli kozala, soki oyebi yango.

## Mibeko mpo na kobongisa bafile

- File moko = vɛrsɛ moko: `<mokanda>/<mokapo>/<vɛrsɛ>.json`.
- **Motuya ya biteni ekokani na motuya ya bandimbola**: liloba ya ebre moko na moko ezwaka ndimbola kaka moko; kolongola te, kobakisa te.
- Elembo **⟨את⟩** elimwisamaka ata moke te; ebakisamaka ata moke te.
- Maloba ya kobakisa etikalaka kati na **⟨⟩**, mpe moke ndenge ekoki.
- **Bankombo etikalaka bankombo**: Yawe, Elohim, El, Eloa, Adonai, Shadai, Tsebaot, Ya — batyaka *Nkolo*, *Nzambe* to *Yehova* na esika na yango te.
- **Sheol**, *lifelo* te; **hesed**, **Mashiah**, **Tora** etikalaka na lokito ya ebre.

## Makomi

- Alphabet ya lingala ya momesano (Latin), kozanga bilembo ya tono; e mpe o ekomamaka mpo na ɛ mpe ɔ.
- Angele mpe français ya polele (Dieu, Seigneur, Éternel) ekotaka te na makomi.

## Mitinda

- Bosembo na lettre eleki petele ya kotanga.
- Kobakisa mateya te; kolongola te oyo makomi elobi.
- Vɛrsɛ na vɛrsɛ na esika ya motangi na yango ya liboso — koyeba-liboso te.

## Mosala na mayele-ya-masini (AI)

Boleka ya liboso ya libongoli oyo esalemaki na mayele-ya-masini likolo ya nzela ya mibeko (docs/methodology/translation-discipline/ln.md na ebombelo ya Selah), mpe loboko ya moto elekaki likolo na yango. Mbeba etikali — yango wana lolenge oyo ya kosangana ezali.

## Ndingisa, lokumu mpe botali

Na kotinda likanisi to bosembolami, ondimi ete mosala na yo ekota na nse ya ndingisa kaka moko ya ebombelo (CC BY-SA 4.0). Bosembolami nyonso etalamaka na miso ya makomi ya ebre; makomi ya ebre nde ekataka.

# Contributing to the Selah Lingala rendering (English)

Readers, students of Hebrew, translators, and every native Lingala speaker are welcome — you know the language better than a machine.

- **Report an error or suggest a fix**: open an Issue or a Pull request at <https://github.com/selah-project/selah-ln>, or write to <support@selahproject.com>. Include the reference (e.g. `genesis 1:1`), what stands now, why it is wrong, and what it should be.
- **Editing rules**: one file per verse (`<book>/<chapter>/<verse>.json`); the token count must equal the gloss count; never remove or add an ⟨את⟩ marker; supplied words stay inside ⟨brackets⟩ and stay few; the Names stay Names (Yawe, Elohim, El, Eloa, Adonai, Shadai, Tsebaot, Ya — never Nkolo, Nzambe, or Yehova in their seat); Sheol, never lifelo; hesed, Mashiah, Tora keep their Hebrew sound.
- **Orthography**: standard practical Lingala (Latin, no tone marks, e/o for ɛ/ɔ); no bare English or French in the text.
- **Principles**: letter-faithfulness outranks readability; add no doctrine, weaken nothing the text says; each verse in its first reader's light.
- The first pass was AI-rendered on the discipline rails and human-checked; errors remain — that is why this door is open. By contributing you agree your work is licensed under the repository license (CC BY-SA 4.0). Every correction is judged against the Hebrew; the Hebrew decides.

## Conduct

Be honest, be kind, show your evidence. Distinguish certainty from
suggestion. The maintainers weigh and decide.
